V71 - COMPANY DEVICE WHITELIST

Device access is now controlled by a company-wide whitelist.

- Admin approves each company laptop/browser device once.
- An Active device is available to any valid EMS employee/admin account.
- Devices are NOT assigned to a specific employee.
- Blocked/unregistered devices cannot reach the EMS dashboard/login flow.
- A new/replacement company laptop must be registered and approved once.
- Personal/unapproved devices remain blocked.

Important: this uses a browser/device registration cookie and user-agent binding, not a MAC address. Browsers do not expose the laptop MAC address to normal web applications. Clearing cookies or switching browser profiles can create a new device ID and require re-registration.
