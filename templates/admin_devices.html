How to register a company laptop

  1. Open EMS on the new company laptop.
  2. Enter a valid EMS username and password.
  3. If the device is new, EMS creates a Pending company device record.
  4. Copy the displayed Device ID and give it to Admin.
  5. Admin approves the device below. No employee assignment is required.
  6. After approval, any authorized employee can use that company laptop.
