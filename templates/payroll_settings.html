Automatic Philippine Statutory Deductions

Payroll uses the statutory tables shown below as its source of truth. The rates and brackets are not entered as flat percentages here. The SSS table is the Business Employers and Employees schedule effective January 2025; PhilHealth is the CY 2025 premium schedule; and Pag-IBIG uses the current membership contribution rates with a maximum monthly compensation basis of ₱5,000.
The payroll engine determines the monthly statutory bases first, then looks up the applicable table/rule. SSS uses total actual remuneration; PhilHealth uses fixed Monthly Basic Salary and excludes overtime, allowances, 13th-month pay, bonuses and deductions caused by undertime/tardiness/leave without pay/absences; Pag-IBIG uses monthly compensation capped at ₱5,000.
