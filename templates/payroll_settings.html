Automatic Philippine Statutory Deductions

SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR withholding tax are now calculated automatically by the payroll engine. Do not enter flat percentage rates here. The engine uses the current statutory tables/rules configured in the application.
For partial attendance/pay, the monthly statutory obligation is calculated from the employee's Basic Daily Rate × working days/month, then prorated to the actual gross paid in the cutoff. This prevents a short payroll from taking a full monthly contribution.
